# StreamFlix — Phase 2 EDA Charts

## Overview

This folder contains the visualization outputs generated during **Phase 2 — Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** of the StreamFlix Content Analytics project.

The charts were created using Python with **Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn** and exported as high-resolution PNG files for use in project documentation, presentations, reports, GitHub, and portfolio demonstrations.

## Chart Descriptions

### 01 — Monthly Viewing Volume

Shows the number of viewing sessions recorded each month.

**Purpose:** Identify monthly viewing patterns and peak viewing periods.

---

### 02 — Monthly Watch Hours

Shows the total number of hours watched each month.

**Purpose:** Measure overall engagement and identify changes in viewing activity over time.

---

### 03 — Genre-wise Watch Hours

Shows the total watch hours generated by each content genre.

**Purpose:** Identify the genres that receive the highest viewer engagement.

---

### 04 — Content Type Split

Compares viewing hours between **Movies** and **TV Shows**.

**Purpose:** Understand which content format contributes more to total viewing time.

---

### 05 — Top 10 Countries by Watch Hours

Ranks the top 10 countries according to total watch hours.

**Purpose:** Identify the strongest geographic markets for StreamFlix.

---

### 06 — Subscriber Plan Distribution

Shows the distribution of subscribers across available subscription plans.

**Purpose:** Understand the composition of StreamFlix's subscriber base.

---

### 07 — Device Usage

Shows the number of viewing sessions by device type.

**Purpose:** Identify the devices most frequently used for streaming.

---

### 08 — Age Distribution

Displays the age distribution of StreamFlix subscribers.

**Purpose:** Understand the demographic profile of the subscriber base.

---

### 09 — Completion Rate by Genre

Compares the average content completion percentage across genres.

**Purpose:** Identify genres with stronger or weaker viewer completion and engagement.

---

### 10 — Review Sentiment Breakdown

Shows the distribution of **Positive, Neutral, and Negative** reviews.

**Purpose:** Understand overall subscriber sentiment and customer feedback.
