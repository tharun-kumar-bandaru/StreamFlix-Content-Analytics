# StreamFlix — Phase 3: KPI Calculations

## Overview

Phase 3 of the StreamFlix Data Analytics project focuses on calculating key business performance indicators (KPIs) that help measure subscriber engagement, retention, revenue, and content performance.

The analysis uses the cleaned datasets prepared during **Phase 1 — Data Quality & Cleaning** and the analytical outputs from **Phase 2 — Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**.

The objective is to transform raw analytical data into meaningful business metrics that can support management and strategic decision-making.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of Phase 3 are to:

- Measure overall platform engagement.
- Analyze active and inactive subscribers.
- Calculate subscriber churn.
- Measure content completion behavior.
- Estimate Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR).
- Calculate Average Revenue Per User (ARPU).
- Measure average viewing time per subscriber.
- Analyze watchlist-to-viewing conversion.
- Identify dependency on high-performing titles.
- Measure engagement generated by original content.
- Analyze subscriber retention through cohort analysis.

## KPIs Calculated

### 1. Total Watch Hours

**Formula:**

```text
SUM(watch_duration_min) ÷ 60
```

Measures the total amount of content watched across the StreamFlix platform.

---

### 2. Active Rate

**Formula:**

```text
Active Subscribers ÷ Total Subscribers × 100
```

Measures the percentage of subscribers who are currently active.

**Target:** > 70%

---

### 3. Churn Rate

**Formula:**

```text
Inactive Subscribers ÷ Total Subscribers × 100
```

Measures the percentage of subscribers who have churned.

**Target:** < 30%

---

### 4. Average Completion Rate

**Formula:**

```text
AVG(completion_pct)
```

Measures the average percentage of content completed by viewers.

**Target:** > 60%

---

### 5. Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)

**Formula:**

```text
SUM(monthly_price)
```

Calculated for active subscribers.

MRR represents the recurring monthly revenue generated by the subscriber base.

---

### 6. Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)

**Formula:**

```text
MRR ÷ Active Subscribers
```

Measures the average recurring revenue generated per active subscriber.

---

### 7. Average Watch Time per Subscriber

**Formula:**

```text
Total Watch Hours ÷ Active Subscribers
```

Measures the average amount of viewing time generated by each active subscriber.

---

### 8. Watchlist Conversion

**Formula:**

```text
Watched Watchlist Titles ÷ Total Watchlist Entries × 100
```

Measures how effectively user viewing intent converts into actual content consumption.

**Target:** > 40%

---

### 9. Hit Concentration

**Formula:**

```text
Plays from Top 10% Titles ÷ Total Plays × 100
```

Measures how much of the platform's viewing activity is concentrated among its most popular titles.

A high concentration may indicate dependency on a small number of successful titles.

---

### 10. Originals Share of Hours

**Formula:**

```text
Hours Watched on Originals ÷ Total Watch Hours × 100
```

Measures the percentage of total viewing generated by StreamFlix original content.

## Bonus Analysis — Cohort Retention

Subscribers are grouped according to their signup month.

Retention is measured at:

- 3 months
- 6 months
- 12 months

This analysis helps identify how effectively StreamFlix retains subscribers over time.

### Cohort Retention Formula

```text
Subscribers Active at Month N
÷
Original Subscribers in Cohort
× 100
```

## Business Value

The KPIs calculated in Phase 3 convert StreamFlix data into actionable business insights.

These metrics help answer important questions such as:

- How engaged are StreamFlix subscribers?
- How many subscribers are currently active?
- How many subscribers are being lost?
- Are users completing the content they watch?
- How much recurring revenue is being generated?
- How much revenue does the average active subscriber generate?
- Are users converting their watchlist intentions into actual viewing?
- Is StreamFlix overly dependent on a few hit titles?
- How important are original productions to overall engagement?
- How well does StreamFlix retain subscribers over time?
